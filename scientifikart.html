ScientiFikART

Science through art.

Researchers shared artistic work connected to their research, including cosmic cross-stitch, moving antennas, visual art and a scientific paper turned into a black box.

The programme included music, poetry, sculpture, textiles and storytelling, with presentations followed by a reception.
