ScientiFikART

When science meets art.

Researchers stepped outside the usual language of science to share artistic work connected to their research: from cosmic cross-stitch and moving antennas to visual art and a scientific paper turned into a black box.

The programme made space for music, poetry, sculpture, textiles, storytelling and more, with presentations followed by a reception.
