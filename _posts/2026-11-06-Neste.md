There are monsters in the cosmos. When they use their Weapons, they summon ghosts. Light often can’t escape their lairs, but the ghosts Get Out. Every so often, one of these ghosts interacts, leaving a smoking gun that points to the most violent places in the universe. At the South Pole, the IceCube Neutrino Observatory uses thousands of sensors frozen deep in the Antarctic ice to catch a Colour Out of Space: a faint blue glow in the dark. This year, Francis Halzen, our head Ghostbuster, was awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics, the reward for a decades-long Obsession. I’ll tell the story of how we catch these ghosts, the cosmic monsters they lead us to, and what it takes to find the Thing from Another World.
