Remember when you decided to take a master's degree because you wanted to become a researcher and/or professor? And then you doubled down that choice when you finally accepted (or will accept!) that PhD position? Most of us, if not all, were brought here to pursue a dream: we wanted to understand nature, we wanted to make a difference, we wanted to teach, we wanted to... you name it - but slowly, though surely, most of us end up leaving academia. So, who are the ones who stay? And how do they manage it? Guess what, I don't have the answers to that! But based on anecdotes and talking to senior researchers, I compiled a few key things that I'd like to share in the hope this might make a difference along your path. As this talk is open for everyone, I will also open the floor at the end for an honest discussion about academia and expectations where everyone is invited to join.
