The Nobel Prize in Physics 2026 has been awarded to Francis Halzen “for decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin”. I will explain the science and technology behind this year’s Nobel Prize in Physics, and outline the process leading up to the Prize announcement.
