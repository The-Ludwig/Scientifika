Good to know.

Who can come?

Most Fridays are for bachelor’s, master’s and PhD students, and postdocs. Events marked “open to everyone” also welcome senior researchers, faculty and staff. Check the audience on the event page.

Do I need to know the subject?

No specialist background in the speaker’s field is expected. Questions are welcome throughout, and student questions are prioritised.

What is fika?

A Swedish coffee break, with something to eat and time to talk. At ScientiFika, it is part of getting to know one another.
