Our story

Built from
the ground up.
Held together
by fika.

ScientiFika brings researchers across Stockholm together to share their science and build a community.

Since 2021, the idea has been simple: make room for accessible talks, informal conversations and connections beyond your own field.

Discover more about us here!

Four pillars.

One shared table.

01

Community

A supportive place for junior researchers to get to know each other.

02

Accessibility & fun

Research explained for people outside the speaker’s own field.

03

Across disciplines

Interesting questions and connections between different areas of research.

04

Mentorship & collaboration

A place to find collaborators, mentors and a path forward.

Room for your questions.

Questions throughout. Student questions prioritised. An informal space to learn from one another.

Junior researchers at the centre

Our usual audience is bachelor’s, master’s and PhD students, and postdocs. Selected events welcome senior researchers, faculty and staff too.

The audience is shown on each event. This balance is deliberate: a supportive peer community, with opportunities to connect across career stages.

Who can join?

What to expect

Accessible talks. Questions while the speaker is talking. Research and career conversations. And fika to keep the conversation going.

A new chapter · April 2026

From Stockholm
to Uppsala.

The first ScientiFika Uppsala event took place on 23 April 2026 with Guilherme Franzmann’s “On the nature of time”.

Our partners at ÅSCO, Ångström SciComm & Outreach, continued the programme in the autumn.
