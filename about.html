Our story

A community
for researchers.
Since 2021.

ScientiFika brings researchers across Stockholm together to share their science and build a community.

We meet for informal talks about research and academic life, with time for questions and fika.

Discover more about us here!

What we aim for

01

Community

Meet junior researchers from other groups and universities.

02

Accessibility & fun

Talks that explain the research without assuming a background in the subject.

03

Across disciplines

Learn about research beyond your own discipline.

04

Mentorship & collaboration

Get to know people you might work with or ask for advice.

Questions and discussion

You can ask questions during the talk. We give priority to students’ questions.

Who comes to ScientiFika?

Our usual audience is bachelor’s, master’s and PhD students, and postdocs. Selected events welcome senior researchers, faculty and staff too.

See each event’s page for its intended audience.

Who can join?

What to expect

Talks cover research and academic life. They are informal, with questions throughout and fika afterwards.
